# Getting started for workspace owners

EloSync is in an invite-only Founding Beta. Access isn't open sign-up: you apply, we review your application, and accepted applicants get an invite link.

## 1. Apply and accept your invite
1. Apply at [elosync.com/beta](https://elosync.com/beta/).
2. If your application is accepted, you'll receive an email with a workspace invite link.
3. Open the link and register with **the same email address you applied with**. Registering with a different email fails.
4. If the link expired or never arrived, go to [elosync.com/beta](https://elosync.com/beta/), open **Already accepted?**, enter your application email and choose **Resend invite**. Resending creates a new link, and the old one stops working.

Invite links expire after 14 days by default.

## 2. Verify your email and sign in
- Verify your email when prompted. Unverified accounts can't open workspace pages.
- Sign in at the shared login page (`/login`). EloSync finds your workspace from your email.
- After sign-in you land on the **Dashboard**.

The person who registers the workspace is its **Owner**, with full workspace access.

## 3. What's in your workspace from day one
Every workspace includes **Leads**, **Tasks** and **ToDos** free.
Many more modules are **free to install** from the Marketplace, such as Contacts, Companies, Calendar, Meetings, Opportunities, Quotations, Projects and Help Desk.
**Branded**, **Automation**, **WhatsApp Cloud** and **AI Assistant** are optional paid add-ons. See [Pricing](https://elosync.com/pricing/).

## 4. Invite your team
1. Open **Administration → Users** and choose **New user**.
2. Enter their name, email and password, and pick one or more roles.
3. Default roles often include Owner, Administrator, Manager and Staff. You can create your own (for example Sales Agent or Finance) under **Administration → Roles**.

If a teammate never gets the verification email, open their row menu and choose **Resend verification** or **Mark as verified**.
See [Tenant RBAC](/user-guide/tenant-rbac).

## 5. Bring in your first leads
- **By hand:** open **Leads**, then **New** (shortcut **N**).
- **From your website or a form tool:** create a webhook under **Leads → Integrations → Webhooks**. See [Connect a lead source](/user-guide/lead-sources).
- **From Meta Lead Ads (Facebook lead forms):** see [Connect a lead source](/user-guide/lead-sources).

Give every lead an owner so each inquiry has a clear person responsible.

## 6. Turn the next step into a task
Open **Tasks**, create a task with an assignee and a due date, and use comments to keep context with the work.
See [Tasks](/user-guide/tasks).

## 7. Add modules when you need them
Open **Settings → Marketplace → Open Marketplace**.
Free modules activate as soon as you install them. If a module needs another one first, the module's detail panel lists it with an **Install** action. For example, Quotations and Contracts need Opportunities.
See [Module dependencies](/user-guide/module-dependencies).
