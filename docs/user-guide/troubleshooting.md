# Troubleshooting

**My invite link expired or never arrived.**
On [elosync.com/beta](https://elosync.com/beta/), open **Already accepted?**, enter the email you applied with, and choose **Resend invite**. The new link replaces the old one. Invite links expire after 14 days by default.

**Registration says "We are not currently accepting new registrations."**
EloSync is invite-only. Use your invite link, or apply at [elosync.com/beta](https://elosync.com/beta/).

**Registration fails with my invite.**
Register with the same email address you applied with.

**A teammate never got the verification email.**
An Owner or Admin opens **Administration → Users**, then the row menu, and chooses **Resend verification** or **Mark as verified**.

**A module won't install.**
It probably needs another module first. See [Module dependencies](/user-guide/module-dependencies).

**I can't see a module or a menu item.**
Your workspace must have the module installed, and your role must include its permission. Ask your Owner or Admin.

**"Delete permanently" is missing.**
It needs a separate force-delete permission, which most default roles don't have.

**I can't upload a file.**
Uploads need the Storage module and remaining storage space.
